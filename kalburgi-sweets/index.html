Gokak Culinary Heritage

The Tradition of Open-Kadhai Karadant at Naka No. 1

Gokak has been renowned for Karadant for generations. At Kalburgi Sweets, we honor that craft by using unrefined local jaggery, pure cow ghee, and hand-sorted California almonds, cashews, pistachios, and dried figs.

We fry edible gum in desi ghee until each crystal puffs into a golden, crisp crunch. Simmering the jaggery in heavy brass kadhais caramelizes it naturally, creating Karadant that is dense, nutritious, and free from synthetic preservatives.

Alongside traditional sweets, our in-house bakery provides fresh birthday and anniversary celebration cakes stored in temperature-controlled display refrigeration. Whether you need an authentic wedding hamper or a fresh cake, our Rani Chennamma Circle counter prepares both with equal care.

Visiting Gokak Falls?

Our shop at Naka No. 1 sits directly on the main highway corridor. We pack all Karadant and laddu orders in sturdy, airtight boxes designed to travel cleanly to Bengaluru, Pune, Mumbai, or Hyderabad without spoiling.
